A Message from the Creator

Twenty Years of Self-Determination

On September 1, 2006, we stopped waiting for permission.

Twenty years ago today, Dancers 4 Dancers was born out of a simple, defiant idea: if New York City’s commercial clubs wouldn't bring Chicago’s DJ FLX to our dance floors, we would build the dance floor ourselves. Alongside my brilliant co-creators Marci Roth, Kenneth Madrigal, and Lauren Cohen, we looked at the historic, raw walls of the Brooklyn Lyceum and saw a blank canvas for our community.

We didn’t just want to throw another party. We wanted to build a sanctuary that respected the dancer as a complete artist. That night became a living, breathing ecosystem fueled by the sonic power of DJ FLX, Wunmi and her 13-piece live band, and DJ Sabine. It was anchored by the voice of DJ Ed Dunn, documented for history by Muema Lombe (BounceFM) and Human, and covered in the brilliant colors of Concep’s live canvas.

I designed every piece of that world—the logo, the flyers, the t-shirts, the podcast covers, the website—because our culture deserves high-level production value. We proved that dancers are not just passive consumers of nightlife; we are the primary architects of it.

Olawale Oladunni

Creator & Creative Director, Dancers 4 Dancers
